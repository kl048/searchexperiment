Instructions

This is a study on economic decision making. The instructions are simple, and if you follow them carefully, you may earn a CONSIDERABLE AMOUNT OF MONEY which will be PAID TO YOU IN CASH at the end of the experiment.

You are in the role of someone who is searching for a wage and will receive a series of wage offers and have to decide which one if any you will accept. That process is called a search and it will be described in detail in a moment. This experiment consists of twenty searches and after all twenty searches are complete, one will be chosen at random to determine your payment. Wages are denoted in Experimental Currency Units (ECUs) with 5 ECU = $1.

Searches

Each search will consist of an unknown number of periods. At the end of a period, there is a 90% chance that there will be another period and a 10% chance that the search will end. Therefore, you do not know how many periods will be in a search, but in any period you can expect there to be about 10 more periods.

You will begin each search not having a wage. During each period of a search, you will receive a wage offer, which will be an integer between 1 and 100 (including 1 and 100), where all integers are equally likely to be the wage offer you receive. This means that there is a 1/100 = 1% chance it is 1, a 1/100 = 1% chance it is 2, a 1/100 = 1% chance it will be 3, and so on.

At the beginning of a job search, you will be asked to state the lowest wage offer that you are willing to accept. This is your reservation wage. In each period, the computer will compare the wage offer to your reservation wage:

  • If the wage offer is equal to or more than your reservation wage, then the computer will automatically accept the offer for you. This will end the search and your earnings for the search will equal the wage offer you accepted.
  • If the wage offer is less than your reservation wage, the computer will automatically reject the offer for you. The computer will then determine if the search continues to the next period.
    • If the search continues to the next period, then the process repeats itself.
    • If the search does not continue, then the search ends and your earnings for the search will be 10.

Please think carefully about your reservation wage because it will affect your payoff and you will not be able to change it during a search although you can set different reservation wages for different searches.

Chatting

You have been paired with another participant and will stay paired with that person for the entire experiment. The two of you can use a chat box in order to chat with each other before the beginning of each search. You can chat about anything that you want, including advice about what reservation wage to set, except that you cannot post messages which identify yourself (such as age, gender, location in the room, etc.) and you cannot use offensive language.

None of the other participants in the lab will be able to see the messages you and the person you are matched with send to each other. For the first five searches, you will have 60 seconds to chat. Beginning with the sixth search, you will have 30 seconds to chat.

Payment Sharing

You and the person you can chat with will equally share your earnings with each other. At the end of the search, you will receive half of your earnings plus half of their earnings. Similarly, they will receive half of your earnings and half of their own earnings.
